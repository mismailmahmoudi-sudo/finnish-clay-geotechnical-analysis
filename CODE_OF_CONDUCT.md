
# Contributor Covenant 3.0 — Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

The Finnish Clay Geotechnical Analysis project is committed to maintaining a respectful, inclusive, and collaborative research environment.

As contributors to this project, developed for the ING8100 course at Polytechnique Montréal, we agree to treat all participants fairly, regardless of their background, identity, experience, or level of expertise.

Our objective is to encourage open communication, academic integrity, and responsible scientific collaboration.

## Encouraged Behaviors

All contributors are encouraged to:

1. Communicate respectfully and constructively.
2. Recognize and respect different opinions and approaches.
3. Provide clear and helpful feedback during code reviews.
4. Accept constructive criticism professionally.
5. Take responsibility for their contributions and modifications.
6. Properly acknowledge scientific sources and previous work.
7. Support transparency and reproducibility in research.
8. Work cooperatively to achieve the project's objectives.

## Restricted Behaviors

The following behaviors are not acceptable within this project:

1. Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or personal attacks.
2. Offensive or disrespectful communication.
3. Deliberate disruption of collaborative activities.
4. Sharing another contributor's private information without permission.
5. Misrepresenting scientific findings or manipulating research results.
6. Plagiarism or failure to acknowledge external sources.
7. Unauthorized modification or deletion of another contributor's work.
8. Any behavior that negatively affects the safety, dignity, or participation of project members.

### Other Restrictions

Contributors must also avoid:

- Impersonating other project members.
- Presenting someone else's contributions as their own.
- Sharing misleading information about the dataset or analysis.
- Using project communication channels for unrelated promotional activities.
- Disclosing confidential information without authorization.

## Reporting an Issue

Any contributor who experiences or observes behavior that may violate this Code of Conduct should report the issue privately to the project maintainers.

Reports should include relevant information about the incident, where appropriate, while respecting the privacy of everyone involved.

If a conflict cannot be resolved within the project team, or involves a project maintainer, the matter may be referred to the ING8100 course instructor through official university communication channels.

Reports will be treated fairly, respectfully, and confidentially to the extent possible.

## Addressing and Repairing Harm

When a violation of this Code of Conduct occurs, project maintainers will consider an appropriate response based on the seriousness and circumstances of the incident.

The following actions may be considered:

### 1. Warning

For a minor or first-time violation, the contributor may receive a private warning explaining the inappropriate behavior and the expected correction.

### 2. Temporarily Limited Activities

For repeated or more serious violations, certain collaborative activities may be temporarily restricted while the issue is addressed.

### 3. Temporary Suspension

For serious or repeated violations, participation in the project's collaborative activities may be temporarily suspended, subject to the available repository permissions and applicable course rules.

### 4. Permanent Restriction

In exceptional cases involving severe or persistent misconduct, continued participation in the repository may be restricted, subject to consultation with the course instructor when appropriate.

Whenever possible, resolving disagreements through respectful communication, acknowledgment of responsibility, and corrective actions will be prioritized.

These measures are guidelines and do not replace the policies of Polytechnique Montréal.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all interactions related to the Finnish Clay Geotechnical Analysis project, including:

- GitHub repository contributions.
- GitHub Issues and Pull Requests.
- Code reviews and discussions.
- Research documentation and data analysis.
- Meetings and communications between project members.
- Other activities directly associated with the ING8100 laboratory.

All contributors are expected to follow these guidelines throughout the project.
